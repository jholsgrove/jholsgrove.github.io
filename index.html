About Me

I am a hard working individual based in West Sussex who strives to continually learn. My earlier career has seen me doing front end web design and development, technical recruitment of Engineering personnel for all Engineering & Scientific disciplines as well as conducting Specialist Mapping roles.

I started my testing career in 2013 using Selenium WebDriver with C# .NET and SpecFlow/Gherkin, developing Behaviour Driven Development automated tests of web applications in parallel, using specification by example. I use a variety of tools including but not limited to Visual Studio, TeamCity, NUnit, Blazor, Azure & Git for source control.

I am currently Head of QA | Senior Test Engineer at Highlight Service Assurance.
